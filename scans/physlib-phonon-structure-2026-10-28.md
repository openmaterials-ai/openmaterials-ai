# Harmonic lattice dynamics and the phonon transport defects

This tranche proves theorems about five thermal-transport formulas and records three catalog defects. Four results are Physlib-anchored, through the crystal structure `Physlib.CondensedMatter.Crystal` or the canonical heat capacity of the Physlib harmonic oscillator; one is a local proof over Mathlib. On integrate/physlib-coverage aa0639b, coverage moves from 20 + 11 local to 24 + 12 local, 36 of 127 operator formulas proved. The bridge uses Physlib 9b583cb6 on Lean 4.35.0. Physlib's `Crystal` supplies the site and translation bookkeeping (sites, cells, the translation action, the site equivalence); the lattice-dynamics content is OMAI-local.

The dispersion result is a statement about the catalog's `D(q)`. On a finite translation group every additive embedding of the cells is zero, so the plane wave cannot be used there directly. The supercell force constants are therefore the folded sums `Phi_BvK(R) = sum_L Phi(R + L)` of the crystal force constants, and at a wavevector commensurate with the supercell (`e^{i q.L} = 1` on the sublattice) the supercell dynamical matrix at the corresponding character equals the catalog `D(q)`. A stable folded supercell then makes `D(q)` positive semidefinite, so `omega^2 >= 0`. Incommensurate wavevectors are the normal case for BTE (a 4x4x4 FC2 supercell with a 19x19x19 kappa mesh), and Fourier interpolation is exact only at commensurate q; nothing is stated at the other mesh points. Einstein springs on `ofUnitCell (ZMod 3) Unit` show the stability hypothesis is satisfiable.

The group velocity holds on a differentiable eigenvector path, away from Gamma: the Hellmann-Feynman theorem now derives the eigenvalue's differentiability, and the catalog expression `e^dagger (dD/dq) e / (2 omega)` is a real number equal to `d omega/dq`. Symmetry-enforced degeneracies are generic (transverse pairs along Gamma-X and Gamma-L), and the eigenvector a code returns there is not on such a path; phonopy projects per direction and phono3py averages over degenerate sets and the q-star. A monatomic chain on `ofUnitCell Z Unit` gives `omega^2 = (2K/M)(1 - cos(q a))` and catalog velocity `a sqrt(K/M) cos(q a / 2)`. Differentiable eigenvector paths exist through degeneracies (Rellich), but the basis a code returns need not lie on one.

## Hypotheses

The hypotheses that carry physics are each necessary: translation invariance and equal mixed partials for the index-permutation symmetry and hence Hermiticity; a unitary phase for Hermiticity; the acoustic sum rule for the zero modes; stability and commensurability for `omega^2 >= 0`; Hermiticity, normalization, path differentiability and `omega^2 > 0` for the velocity; positive linewidths, `T > 0` and `N_q V_cell > 0` for the RTA tensor; normalization for the participation ratio. Positive masses is a domain condition; with Lean's conventions for square roots and division some conclusions survive non-positive masses, which have no physical meaning. After review the unitarity hypothesis of the Bloch and stability theorems (a Mathlib fact on a finite group) and the eigenvalue-differentiability hypothesis of Hellmann-Feynman were removed, and the Mathlib restatements of the spectral theorem and of PSD-implies-nonnegative eigenvalues were deleted rather than cited. Helpers stay in the modules uncited; a compiler probe checks that every cited Physlib declaration is reached from the cited theorems.

## Conventions

The catalog and the Lean definitions use the cell-vector phase `e^{+i q.R}`. Phonopy and phono3py use atom positions, `D_ph = U^dagger D_cat U` with `U = diag(e^{i q.tau_kappa})`: `omega^2` and nondegenerate velocities agree, eigenvectors differ by `e^{-i q.tau}`, off-diagonal velocity-operator elements differ, and phonopy eigenvectors fed into the catalog's `dD/dq` give wrong velocities. Stored linear THz frequencies need `omega = 2 pi 10^12 nu`; velocities stored in Angstrom THz are already `d omega/dq` (1 Angstrom THz = 100 m/s, no 2 pi). The RTA `Gamma` is the half-width `Im Sigma` (phono3py's gamma), `1/tau = 2 Gamma`; ShengBTE and kALDo report `1/tau`. The participation ratio uses mass-weighted eigenvectors (kALDo), which differs from the displacement-based Bell-Dean value in multi-species crystals, and measures spread over basis atoms at fixed q.

## Catalog defects

`apply_nac_correction`: the wavevector enters as the free component `q^alpha`, `q^beta` instead of the contracted `q_gamma`, `q_delta`, so the term is independent of q; it also lacks `e^2` and ties alpha and beta to nothing. The q -> 0 form is `D^NA_{kappa alpha, kappa' beta} = (4 pi e^2 / Omega) (sum_gamma q_gamma Z*_{kappa, gamma alpha}) (sum_delta q_delta Z*_{kappa', delta beta}) / ((sum q eps_inf q) sqrt(M_kappa M_kappa'))` in Gaussian units (`4 pi e^2 -> e^2/eps_0` in SI), with Gonze-Lee Ewald sums at finite q.

`solve_bte[bte_solver=direct_inverse]`: the catalog collision matrix `M = (2 Gamma/hbar) delta - Xi` (omai/thermal_transport/operator/edges.py) has the wrong RTA limit (it gives `F = hbar c v/(2 Gamma)`, not `v/(2 Gamma)`), is dimensionally inconsistent, and is not symmetric (energy deltas and `q'' = q - q'` in the absorption channel instead of `q + q'`). A symmetric weighting is `M = C^(1/2) Omega~ C^(1/2)`, with `Omega~` the detailed-balance-symmetric collision matrix in rate units and `C = diag(c)`; its RTA limit is `diag(2 Gamma c)`. `lbteKappa_posSemidef` proves the conductivity symmetric positive semidefinite whenever `M` is, and stays uncited until the catalog is fixed; `contract_kappa[bte_solver=direct_inverse]` stays a gap.

`contract_cumulative_kappa[wrt=omega]` and `[wrt=mfp]`: they take the linearized-BTE `F`, for which `kappa_cum` is a signed partial sum, so the catalog's "saturates" and "median mean free path" semantics fail. `tp_cumulative_omega_not_monotone` and `tp_cumulative_mfp_not_monotone` state `¬ Monotone` for a three-mode linearized BTE with `omega = (1, 2, 3)`, one coalescence process `1 + 2 <-> 3`, Casimir scattering `1/tau = |v|/L`, `v_x = (2, 2, -1)` and Bose-Einstein weights, for every `P L >= 10`; the facts used about the weights `n(n+1)` at x = 1, 2, 3 reduce to `E^4 - E^2 + 1 > 0`, `E^2 - E + 1 > 0` and `E > 2` for `E = e`. At `P = 10`, `L = 100` the numbers are kappa = 210.7, cumulative by omega 156.2, 245.0, 210.7, and by |F| 88.8, 54.5, 210.7. `cumulativeKappa_mono` (monotone when every modal contribution is nonnegative, as in RTA) stays uncited.

`identity_dm`, `sum_linewidths` and `solve_bte[bte_solver=rta]` are definitions with nothing to prove and stay unbound.

## Prose for site and paper

Home page and Lean page: 24 of 127 operator formulas carry a Physlib-anchored compiled proof and 12 more a local proof over Mathlib, 36 in all.

Paper, after the composites and cure paragraph: The lattice-dynamics layer uses Physlib's crystal structure for sites and translations. Force constants with equal mixed partials, translation invariance and finite range satisfy `Phi_ij(-R) = Phi_ji(R)`, the catalog dynamical matrix is Hermitian, and the acoustic sum rule gives three zero modes at Gamma. Folding the force constants into a Born-von Karman supercell reproduces the catalog `D(q)` at commensurate wavevectors, where a mechanically stable supercell makes every `omega^2` nonnegative; incommensurate BTE meshes are not covered. On a differentiable eigenvector path away from Gamma the catalog group velocity equals `d omega/dq`. With Physlib canonical heat capacities the relaxation-time conductivity is symmetric and positive semidefinite. Three catalog formulas are defective: the non-analytic correction is independent of q, the linearized-BTE collision matrix is not symmetric and has the wrong relaxation-time limit, and the cumulative conductivity of the linearized BTE is not a distribution function, as an exact three-mode example with a three-phonon process and Casimir scattering shows.
