What MatterGen is

MatterGen is a diffusion model that generates inorganic crystal structures, optionally conditioned on composition, symmetry or target properties such as band gap or bulk modulus. On the map it serves the generated-structure quantity.

In more detail

A diffusion process built for crystals

A crystal is a unit cell with atom types A, fractional coordinates X and lattice L. Each has its own corruption process. Coordinates diffuse under a wrapped normal that respects periodicity and tends to uniform, with the noise scaled by cell size. The lattice diffuses in a symmetric form toward a cubic cell at the average atomic density of the training data. Atom types diffuse in categorical space toward a masked state. The score network outputs invariant scores for types and equivariant scores for coordinates and lattice, so the symmetries are not learned from data. Fine-tuning adds adapter modules in every layer and uses classifier-free guidance at sampling time (Zeni 2025).

Numerics and scale

Of 1,024 generated structures relaxed with DFT, 78% lie within 0.1 eV/atom of the Materials Project hull and 75% within 0.1 eV/atom of the Alex-MP-ICSD hull. For 95% of structures the RMSD to the DFT-relaxed geometry is below 0.076 A. Uniqueness is 100% at 1,000 samples and 52% at 10 million, and 61% of samples are new. Trained on MP-20 only, MatterGen-MP yields 60% more SUN structures than CDVAE and DiffCSP with 50% lower RMSD. Scaling the training set to Alex-MP-20 adds another 70% in SUN rate and cuts RMSD five times. In quinary systems it reached the hull with 10,240 samples against about 70,000 for substitution and 600,000 for random structure search (Zeni 2025).

Data and code

Training structures come from the Materials Project v2022.10.28 and Alexandria, both CC BY 4.0, and the test reference adds ICSD release 2023.1. The MatterSim force field pre-relaxes the generated candidates and screens them for phonon stability before DFT. The microsoft/mattergen repository holds the source code, the Alex-MP datasets and the CIF files of the paper. On the map MatterGen serves the generated-structure quantity, batches of unit cells written to CIF (Zeni 2025).

Validation

The bulk modulus models were sampled at 50, 100, 150 and 200 GPa, 8,192 candidates each. Among them 101 matched experimentally verified ICSD structures absent from training, and DFT moduli for 95 of these agree with the targets to an MAE of 23 GPa and an RMSE of 32 GPa. One of four synthesis candidates, TaCr2O6, formed with 98% phase purity by Rietveld refinement. With a budget of 180 DFT property calculations the model found 18 SUN structures above 0.2 A^-3 magnetic density and 106 above 400 GPa bulk modulus, against 40 for screening. The jointly fine-tuned magnet model rediscovered 67 synthesised disordered ICSD structures (Zeni 2025).
