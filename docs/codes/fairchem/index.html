What fairchem is

fairchem is the FAIR Chemistry library of machine-learned interatomic potentials. Its training sets include OC20 and OC22 for catalysis and OMat24 for bulk materials. It provides pretrained models and a calculator interface for energies, forces and stresses. On the map it serves the quantities those models predict.

In more detail

Datasets and tasks

OC20 places one of 82 adsorbates on a random low Miller index facet of a stable Materials Project bulk and relaxes it in VASP with RPBE until forces fall below 0.03 eV/A, with subsurface atoms fixed. Adsorption energies are referenced to the relaxed slab and gas-phase molecules. The training set holds 640,081 relaxations and over 133 million structures, with validation and test splits for unseen adsorbates, unseen catalysts and both (Chanussot 2021). OC22 samples 4,286 oxides, enumerates terminations up to Miller index 3, removes some surface oxygens and places O, H, N, C, OH, OOH, H2O, CO or O2 adsorbates at several coverages, all relaxed with PBE+U and spin polarization (Tran 2023).

Numerics and scale

The OC20 baselines use 128, 1024 and 192 hidden channels for CGCNN, SchNet and DimeNet++, 3.6, 7.4 and 1.8 million parameters, graphs cut at 6 A with up to 50 neighbours, and a loss that sums the energy error and the per-structure mean force error. The best in-domain S2EF force MAE is 0.0280 eV/A for a force-only DimeNet++-Large, and the best energy MAE is 0.3585 eV for an energy-only DimeNet++ (Chanussot 2021). OC22 cost over 240 million core-hours. On its S2EF-Total test, GemNet-OC with a 12 A cutoff reaches 0.374 eV in-domain energy MAE and 0.029 eV/A force MAE, ahead of SchNet, DimeNet++, ForceNet, SpinConv, PaiNN and GemNet-dT (Tran 2023).

Interfaces and code

pymatgen enumerates the surfaces, and CatKit and ASE place the adsorbates. The Open-Catalyst-Dataset repository holds all source for dataset generation. The ocp repository publishes baseline models, data loaders and training scripts, and its MODELS.md lists pretrained OC20 checkpoints (Chanussot 2021, Tran 2023). On the map fairchem serves energy, forces and stress through FAIRChemCalculator, the ASE calculator that wraps a pretrained predictor.

Validation

OC20 scores models by energy MAE, force MAE on free atoms, force cosine and EFwT, the fraction of structures with energy within 0.02 eV and all forces within 0.03 eV/A of DFT. ADwT judges relaxed structures by the fraction within a distance threshold of the DFT geometry (Chanussot 2021). On OC22, GemNet-OC reproduces published O, H and OH adsorption energies on rutile surfaces with at least 75% linear correlation. Its OOH versus OH Gibbs scaling has slope 0.73 and intercept 3.44 eV against the literature 0.73 and 3.49 eV. Fine-tuning from OC20 improves energy MAE at every fraction of OC22 training data (Tran 2023).
