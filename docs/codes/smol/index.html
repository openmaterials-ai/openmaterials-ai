What smol is

smol fits cluster expansions to first-principles energies of configurations on a lattice and samples them by Monte Carlo. It is used for configurational thermodynamics of alloys and ionic conductors. On the map it serves the quantities it fits and samples.

In more detail

Formalism

A configuration is a string of occupation variables, one per site of an N-site supercell, and any lattice model is a sum of cluster functions over subsets of sites. smol writes a symmetry-invariant model as H(sigma) = sum over beta of m_beta J_beta Theta_beta(sigma), with crystallographic multiplicities m_beta, coefficients J_beta and correlation functions Theta_beta that average cluster product functions over orbits of symmetry-equivalent clusters. Unlike the classical cluster expansion, the set of correlation functions need not be a basis: any complete set spanning the invariant subspace, redundant or not, is allowed (Barroso-Luque 2022).

Design and fitting

smol is pure Python with a few Cython components, object oriented along the mathematical abstractions, and lean in dependencies, with structure handling and regression from other packages. It is the only cluster expansion package built on pymatgen, which supplies structures and symmetries and links it to FireWorks and atomate. Against the C++ packages ATAT and CASM and the Python package icet, its stated aim is fast development of new methodology (Barroso-Luque 2022). Xie 2024 fitted coefficients by lasso with the penalty from fivefold cross-validation on 64, 134 and 96 training structures for BCC, FCC and gamma-brass Ag-Li, reaching RMSE of 3.3, 1.5 and 0.5 meV/atom with smol and sparse-lm.

Sampling and thermodynamics

smol.moca samples canonical and semigrand canonical ensembles with interchangeable proposal, ensemble, trace and kernel objects (Barroso-Luque 2022). Xie 2024 annealed Ag-Li cells from 10000 K to 20 K with 250,000 to 1,000,000 Metropolis steps per temperature and integrated the average energy over beta for the configurational free energy, adding Phonopy phonon terms. At 200 K four phases are stable, at 300 K only FCC Ag and the BCC solid solution. Jun 2024 drew low-energy Li/vacancy orderings of Li3PS4 from smol Monte Carlo for its local occupancy analysis.
