What DiffCSP is

DiffCSP generates crystal structures with a diffusion model that denoises lattice and fractional coordinates together, keeping the periodic and rotational symmetries of the crystal. Given a composition it proposes candidate structures for later evaluation. On the map it serves the generated-structure quantity.
