What DiffCSP is

DiffCSP generates crystal structures with a diffusion model that denoises lattice and fractional coordinates together, keeping the periodic and rotational symmetries of the crystal. Given a composition it proposes candidate structures for later evaluation. On the map it serves the generated-structure quantity.

In more detail

Periodic E(3) invariance

The distribution p(L, F | A) must be invariant to permutations, to orthogonal transformations of the lattice and to periodic translations of the fractional coordinates. DiffCSP builds in each separately. The lattice L follows a DDPM with a cosine variance schedule and a prior N(0, I). The coordinates F follow score matching with a wrapped normal transition and an exponential noise schedule from 0.005 to 0.5, initialised from U(0, 1). The denoising network takes L^T L and the Fourier transform of relative fractional coordinates. It outputs the lattice score as L times an MLP of pooled features, and one score per node. Three propositions prove the result periodic E(3) invariant (Jiao 2023).

Numerics and scale

The MP-20 model has 6 layers and 512 hidden states with 256 Fourier features and trains for 1,000 epochs, 5.2 hours on a GeForce RTX 3090. Generating 20 candidates for every MP-20 test structure takes 18.4 GPU hours against 95.2 for CDVAE, because DiffCSP needs 1,000 steps where CDVAE needs 5,000. With 20 samples the match rates are 98.60% on Perov-5, 88.47% on Carbon-24, 77.93% on MP-20 and 34.02% on MPTS-52, with RMSE 0.0128, 0.2192, 0.0492 and 0.1749 (Jiao 2023).

Data and interfaces

Training and test data are the CDVAE splits of Perov-5 (18,928 perovskites), Carbon-24 (10,153 carbon structures), MP-20 (45,231 Materials Project structures) and the chronological MPTS-52 (40,476 structures). Lattices are Niggli reduced before training. The StructureMatcher of pymatgen scores the matches. The code is at github.com/jiaor17/DiffCSP (Jiao 2023). On the map DiffCSP serves the generated-structure quantity, candidate cells written as CIF files for later evaluation.

Validation

Ablations on MP-20 with one sample test each component. Joint diffusion gives a 51.49% match rate, generating the lattice first then coordinates 50.03%, coordinates first 36.73%. Removing the L^T L inner product drops the rate to 1.66%. Replacing the wrapped normal by a Gaussian gives 34.09% and dropping the Fourier transform 29.15%. Multi-graph edges in place of the fully connected graph give 25.85% to 28.05%. In ab initio generation DiffCSP matches CDVAE on validity and coverage and lowers the property statistic distances on MP-20 from 0.6875 to 0.3502 for density and from 0.2778 to 0.1247 for formation energy (Jiao 2023).
