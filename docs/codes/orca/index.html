What ORCA is

ORCA is an electronic structure package for molecules: DFT, coupled cluster, multireference and spectroscopic methods, with frequency and property calculations. It is free for academic use under its own license. On the map it serves the molecular quantities it computes.

What it computes on the map

5 quantities, each with the interface the representation records. The link opens the quantity on the map.

  • Forcesminus the Cartesian energy gradient of an ORCA EnGrad run, Hartree/bohr (served in eV/A)eV_per_A
  • HOMOLUMOGapLUMO minus HOMO energy from the ORBITAL ENERGIES block of the ORCA output, eVev
  • MolecularFrequencyVIBRATIONAL FREQUENCIES block of the ORCA output, cm^-1, imaginary modes printed negative, with IR intensities in km/mol from the IR SPECTRUM blockinverse_cm
  • ReactionBarrier[construction=neb_mep]E(TS) - E(reactant) from two ORCA total energies, the TS a saddle with exactly one imaginary modeev
  • TotalEnergyFINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY in the ORCA output, Hartree (served in eV)ev
