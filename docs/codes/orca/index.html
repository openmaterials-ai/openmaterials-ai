What ORCA is

ORCA is an electronic structure package for molecules: DFT, coupled cluster, multireference and spectroscopic methods, with frequency and property calculations. It is free for academic use under its own license. On the map it serves the molecular quantities it computes.

In more detail

Electronic structure methods

ORCA covers semi-empirical methods, Hartree-Fock, DFT with LDA, GGA, meta-GGA, hybrid, double-hybrid and range-separated functionals, and wavefunction methods for single and multireference problems (Neese 2022). Local correlation uses pair natural orbitals in large domains. This family includes DLPNO-CCSD(T) with F12 for open shells, DLPNO-MP2 analytic second derivatives for polarizabilities and NMR shieldings, and DLPNO-STEOM coupled cluster for excited states. CASPT2 and NEVPT2 were reformulated to avoid the four-particle density, next to DLPNO-NEVPT2-F12 and a fully internally contracted MRCCSD. Solvation enters through CPCM and SMD, up to coupled-cluster energies.

Numerics and scale

Version 5.0 rebuilt the integral layer around SHARK, a C++ engine up to 5 to 6 times faster than traditional algorithms for high angular momentum integrals. SHARK now drives analytic Hessians, TD-DFT and response properties about 5 to 10 times faster than before (Neese 2022). RIJCOSX is the default for hybrid functionals. On 16 cores of an AMD EPYC 7302 machine the Fock builds for Vancomycin (176 atoms) ran 14 to 15 times faster than on one core, and about 25 times on 32 cores. The B3LYP/def2-TZVP analytic Hessian of Penicillin (42 atoms) fell from 17,925 s in ORCA 4.2.1 to 1008 s. The new grids are 2 to 4 times larger than the old defaults, but integration still scales linearly.

Licensing and interfaces

ORCA is free of charge for academic users and sold to industry through FAccTs, with more than 40,000 registered academic users and a manual of over 1300 pages (Neese 2022). The compound scripting language automates protocols such as reoptimizing along an imaginary mode until a true minimum is found, and exposes more than 250 properties through the ASCII property file. QM/MM reads CHARMM, AMBER and OpenFF force fields, ONIOM nests up to three layers, and Crystal-QMMM embeds a cluster in converged point charges.

Validation

The grid incompleteness errors of DefGrid2 are 0.01 kcal/mol in relative energies (0.05 with COSX), 0.1 per cm in analytic harmonic frequencies (0.04 with COSX) and below 0.001 eV in excitation energies, and DefGrid3 cuts them by a further factor 2 to 5 (Neese 2022). With the new grids the RIJCOSX results are nearly indistinguishable from fully analytic exchange. In the application papers, ORCA CASSCF D tensors give [(tpaPh)Fe]- an effective barrier of 19.7 per cm against a measured 26 per cm (Lunghi 2017). The computed relaxation time of VO(acac)2 starts to converge to experiment above 5 T (Lunghi 2019).
