What AtomisticSkills mat-diffusion-analysis is

mat-diffusion-analysis is one of the AtomisticSkills, packaged calculations an agent can call. It takes molecular dynamics trajectories at several temperatures, extracts the diffusivity at each from the mean squared displacement, and fits a weighted Arrhenius law through them. The slope gives the activation energy in eV. On the map it serves the diffusivity and the activation energy.

In more detail

Einstein diffusivity and a weighted Arrhenius fit

analyze_diffusion.py converts the ASE frames after the ignore_ps skip into pymatgen structures and calls DiffusionAnalyzer.from_structures with smoothed=False and step_skip=1, using the frame interval read from the MD .log file. The diffusivity of the chosen species comes from the slope of the mean squared displacement, in cm^2/s, with its standard deviation from the structural variance of the MSD. calculate_activation_energy.py collects md_*K/diffusion_results.json and calls pymatgen's fit_arrhenius with the per-temperature errors. It fits the Arrhenius law D(T) = D0 exp(-E_a/k_B T) as a weighted line in ln D against 1/T.

Numerics and checks

The SKILL.md asks for 4 to 6 temperatures, typically between 600 and 1200 K, supercells longer than 10 A in every direction and more than 20 mobile ions for the statistics. The user should check the MSD for a linear regime after the equilibration skip, 5.0 ps by default. The fitting script prints R^2 and warns below 0.95. It propagates the uncertainties through the covariance matrix of the weighted fit into E_a and into the diffusivity and ionic conductivity extrapolated to 300 K through pymatgen's get_extrapolated_conductivity, which takes the structure file given by --structure. In the LGPS example this gives 0.152 +/- 0.035 eV and 91.44 +/- 79.18 mS/cm from five 20 ps runs.

Interfaces and environment

Trajectories must be ASE .traj files, produced by the run_md tool of a potential skill such as mace or matgl, which expands the cell to supercell_min_length (10 A by default) and can run several structures at once. The diffusion monitor from mat-md-monitors stops a run when D has converged within a threshold and saves diffusion_{specie}.json and msd_{specie}.png itself, so step 2 is skipped for those runs. The analysis scripts run in the cpu environment through venv/run. The sibling skill mat-md-monitors documents the monitors.
