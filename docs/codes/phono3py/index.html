What phono3py is

phono3py computes phonon-phonon interaction strengths from third-order force constants, obtained by finite displacements with an external force calculator. From them it evaluates phonon lifetimes and the lattice thermal conductivity, by the relaxation-time approximation or by solving the linearized Boltzmann equation. On the map it serves the third-order force constants, the lifetimes and the conductivities.

In more detail

Force constants from supercells

Second and third order force constants come from the supercell approach with finite atomic displacements of 0.03 A. The crystal symmetry selects the first displaced atom and the site symmetry left after the first selects the second. Applying the symmetry operations to the computed elements recovers the full tensors. In the 2015 study the harmonic constants used 4x4x4 supercells of the conventional cell (512 atoms) for zincblende and 5x5x3 (300 atoms) for wurtzite, and the cubic constants 2x2x2 (64 atoms) and 3x3x2 (72 atoms). The non-analytical term correction adds the long-range Coulomb part from Born charges and the dielectric tensor (Togo 2015).

Linewidths, grids and the tetrahedron method

The phonon lifetime is the reciprocal of twice the imaginary part of the self energy, computed from the phonon-phonon interaction strength by Fermi's golden rule. Lifetimes were sampled on 96x96x96 and 80x80x48 meshes for the band structures and 19x19x19 and 19x19x15 meshes for the conductivities (Togo 2015). phono3py uses a Gamma-centred regular grid so that q and q' pairs map onto the grid, and reduces them by the crystallographic point group, time reversal and permutation symmetry. It integrates the energy-conserving deltas with the linear tetrahedron method, implemented as a module that returns weights per grid point (Togo 2023).

RTA and the direct solution

Under the single-mode relaxation time approximation kappa is a sum over irreducible q points of heat capacity times the outer product of group velocities times lifetime, weighted by the number of branches in the star of q (Togo 2023). The direct solution of the linearized Boltzmann equation follows Chaput's method. For the 33 compounds it gives values close to the RTA. The exceptions at 300 K with isotope scattering include BAs, 1.23e3 by RTA versus 2.94e3 W/m/K, and GaN, 1.54e2 versus 1.81e2 (Togo 2015). Isotope scattering enters as a separate rate from the mass variance of each element (Togo 2015).

Validation

Table IV of the 2015 paper compares LBTE values with isotopes to room temperature experiments: BN 7.26e2 against 7.6e2, BP 4.20e2 against 3.5e2, GaP 1.04e2 against 1.00e2, InP 85.2 against 93, GaAs 32.1 against 45 W/m/K. Zincblende and wurtzite polytypes of the same composition give similar conductivities, densities of states and linewidth distributions (Togo 2015). The random displacement scheme in the 2023 paper is valid only when every commensurate mode is dynamically stable, and for strongly anharmonic phases the authors recommend renormalized frequencies from self-consistent phonon calculations (Togo 2023).
