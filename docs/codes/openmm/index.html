What OpenMM is

OpenMM is a molecular dynamics engine with a Python API and GPU kernels. Biomolecular simulation is its main use, and it also runs machine-learned potentials. It provides integrators, thermostats, barostats and custom forces. On the map it serves the quantities obtained from its trajectories.

In more detail

Architecture and forces

OpenMM is a layered library. Python classes sit on top, custom forces and integrators in the middle, and computational kernels loaded as plugins at the bottom, so one script runs on a CPU or a GPU (Eastman 2017). Standard forces include Lennard-Jones, PME and generalized Born solvent. Ten custom force classes, from CustomBondForce to CustomManyParticleForce, accept an algebraic expression, and CustomIntegrator writes an integration algorithm the same way. AMOEBA uses spherical harmonics in a quasi-internal frame with DIIS or extrapolated polarization for the induced dipoles, and Drude particles offer a second polarizable model. OpenMM 8 adds TorchForce and OpenMM-ML (Eastman 2023).

Numerics and scale

With AMBER99SB-ILDN, TIP3P and PME, DHFR (23,558 atoms) runs at 367 ns/day on a Titan X Pascal and 13.0 ns/day on four Core i7 cores at a 2 fs step, and 706 ns/day at 5 fs with hydrogen mass repartitioning (Eastman 2017). Both GPUs of one K80 run a simulation 22 to 62 percent faster than one GPU. Single and mixed precision run at similar speed while double precision drops to 18.1 ns/day on the Titan X. In OpenMM 8 the HIP backend reaches 1031 ns/day for DHFR on an AMD V620, four NVLink A100 GPUs run the 1,067,095-atom satellite tobacco mosaic virus at 70.9 ns/day, and NNPOps lifts ANI-2x on a 53-atom inhibitor from 9.8 to 56 ns/day, against 281 ns/day for OpenFF 2.0.0 (Eastman 2023).

Interfaces and related packages

Systems arrive from PDB files with XML force fields or from Amber, CHARMM, Gromacs and Desmond input files, so users keep their tools (Eastman 2017). The code is developed on GitHub under the openmm organization and available from openmm.org. OpenMM-Torch bridges to PyTorch, OpenMM-ML wraps ANI-1ccx and ANI-2x, and the SPICE dataset of omega-B97M-D3BJ/def2-TZVPPD energies and forces over 15 elements is meant to train further potentials (Eastman 2023). OpenFF and GAFF handle small molecules. On the map, run_md.py reads potential energy through getPotentialEnergy(), temperature through StateDataReporter, the pressure setpoint from MonteCarloBarostat and the trajectory through DCDReporter.

Validation

In 1 ns constant-energy runs of DHFR repeated ten times, the energy drift is 1.557 kJ/mol/ps in single precision, -0.0047 in mixed and -0.0062 in double. Mixed precision gives a very large benefit at low cost (Eastman 2017). Forces and energies for DHFR and the 2KOC RNA hairpin loaded through each input pipeline were compared with Amber 16, Gromacs 4.6.5 and CHARMM-LITE c40b1. Production pipelines use the code. AlphaFold relaxes its predictions in Amber99sb with OpenMM 7.3.1 (Jumper 2021), ColabFold relaxes in Amber force fields and cites OpenMM 7 (Mirdita 2022), and ProMod3 in SWISS-MODEL ends with an energy minimization in OpenMM with CHARMM27 (Waterhouse 2018).
