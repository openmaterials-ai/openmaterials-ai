What AMSET is

AMSET computes electronic transport in semiconductors from DFT inputs. It evaluates scattering rates for acoustic and polar optical phonons, ionized impurities and piezoelectric coupling, then solves for mobility, conductivity and Seebeck coefficient as a function of temperature and doping. On the map it serves the electronic transport quantities that depend on those rates.

In more detail

Scattering without DFPT

AMSET writes each mechanism as a Born-approximation matrix element. Acoustic deformation potential scattering joins the Bardeen-Shockley form to the Khan-Allen deformation potential tensor, with each mode's strain from the Christoffel equation and amplitude from kB T over rho c^2. Piezoelectric scattering uses the full piezoelectric stress tensor and all three modes. Polar optical scattering extends the Frohlich model with overlaps, an anisotropic permittivity and one dipole-weighted phonon frequency. Ionized impurities are Coulomb potentials screened by the full dielectric tensor. Elastic rates use the momentum relaxation time approximation, polar rates the self-energy one (Ganose 2021).

Numerics and scale

Eigenvalues and group velocities are Fourier interpolated onto dense grids with BoltzTraP2, and AMSET evaluates scattering only at the most important k-points. A modified linear tetrahedron integration resamples cross sections with hundreds of points that satisfy the delta function exactly and reproduces the Brooks-Herring mobility for parabolic bands. Matrix element cost does not depend on the number of atoms. For NbFeSb and Ba2BiAu the full mobility took 29 core hours against 8350 for DFPT plus Wannier, and ten temperatures for Ba2BiAu under 35 core hours against about 32,000. The transport step for each material ran in under an hour on a laptop (Ganose 2021).

Inputs and interfaces

In the paper the first-principles inputs come from VASP with PBE, managed by atomate. The runs use a 520 eV cutoff, 64 k-points/A^3 for relaxation and 1000 k-points/A^3 for the uniform run. DFPT gives the dielectric and piezoelectric tensors, and stress-strain fits give the elastic constants. A scissor shifts band gaps to HSE06. The Materials Project already holds over 3300 piezoelectric tensors, 7100 dielectric constants and 13,000 elastic tensors. AMSET is a Python package with command-line tools (Ganose 2021). On the map it serves carrier mobility, electrical conductivity, electronic thermal conductivity, Seebeck coefficient and the static dielectric tensor assembled from the DFPT inputs.

Validation

Mode-resolved rates for n-Si and n-GaAs at 300 K match EPW and PERTURBO, including the onset of polar optical emission in GaAs. Across 21 semiconductors from 2 to 48 atoms per cell, calculated mobilities at 300 K span ZnO at 180 cm^2/(V s) to n-GaAs at 2.1e4 cm^2/(V s) with a Spearman rank of 0.93 against experiment, where a 10 fs constant lifetime gives 0.52. The mean squared error of the temperature dependence is 0.20 against 0.19 for DFPT plus Wannier and 3.2 for a constant lifetime. CH3NH3PbI3 agrees with EPW within 5% at all temperatures. AMSET overestimates n-ZnS about 4 times because PBE gives an effective mass of 0.16 against 0.22 measured. HSE06 fixes it (Ganose 2021).
