What AtomisticSkills mat-surface-adsorption is

mat-surface-adsorption is one of the AtomisticSkills, packaged calculations an agent can call. It wraps matcalc's AdsorptionCalc: slabs are cut from the bulk, the adsorbate is placed on each candidate site, slab and adsorbate-on-slab are relaxed with the chosen ASE calculator, and the adsorption energy is E(adslab) minus E(slab) minus E(adsorbate), per site. On the map it serves the adsorption energy.
