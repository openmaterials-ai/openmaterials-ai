What AtomisticSkills mat-surface-adsorption is

mat-surface-adsorption is one of the AtomisticSkills, packaged calculations an agent can call. It wraps matcalc's AdsorptionCalc: slabs are cut from the bulk, the adsorbate is placed on each candidate site, slab and adsorbate-on-slab are relaxed with the chosen ASE calculator, and the adsorption energy is E(adslab) minus E(slab) minus E(adsorbate), per site. On the map it serves the adsorption energy.

In more detail

One site at a time on a relaxed slab

The skill wraps matcalc's AdsorptionCalc and its calc_adslabs call. It relaxes the bulk, cuts a slab for the requested Miller index given as a JSON list such as [1,1,1], and relaxes the clean slab and the isolated adsorbate on their own. AdsorptionCalc then places the adsorbate on each ontop, bridge and hollow site, 0.9 A above the surface by default, and relaxes every adsorbate-slab configuration. The adsorption energy of a site is E(adsorbate+slab) - E(slab) - E(adsorbate). A negative value is exothermic, and the SKILL.md gives -0.5 to -5.0 eV as the usual range for strong chemisorption.

Numerics and cost

The relaxations use ASE's BFGS to fmax 0.05 eV/A with at most 500 steps. The slab holds at least 10 A of material and 20 A of vacuum, which the SKILL.md calls critical against periodic-image interactions, and layered materials may need a thicker slab. Cost scales with the number of sites and the slab size, so --adsorption_sites ontop or bridge trims the search. The bulk should be a well-relaxed crystal and the adsorbate a gas-phase molecule, not a periodic structure.

Potentials and environment

The SKILL.md ranks potentials for this task. Open Catalyst models are first: FAIRChem EquiformerV2-31M and 153M S2EF-OC20-All+MD, UMA uma-s-1p1 and uma-m-1p1, and MACE-OMAT-0-small and medium. MatPES models such as CHGNet-PES-MatPES-PBE-1M-2026.9 and TensorNet-PES-MatPES-PBE-2025.2 come second. It advises against models trained only on MPtrj because of their force errors. The command runs through venv/run in the fairchem environment for FairChem models and in mlip for MACE and MatGL. The sibling skill mat-surface-energy computes surface energies and Wulff shapes.
