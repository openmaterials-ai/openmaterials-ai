reaction-network: reaction pathways through a graph of solid-state reactions

Builds a weighted graph of balanced reactions among phases from thermochemistry data and searches it for low-cost multi-step routes to a target phase.

What it is and how it works

Phase space as a graph

Nodes are combinations of up to n phases, and edges are balanced reactions weighted by a softplus cost of the Gibbs energy per reactant atom. The C-Cl-Li-Mn-O-Y network had 5855 nodes and 121,176 edges (McDermott 2021).

Thermochemistry inputs

Formation enthalpies from the Materials Project, Gibbs energies from the Bartel descriptor, elemental G from FactSage, gases and A2CO3 from NIST-JANAF (McDermott 2021).

Softplus cost

C = ln(1 + exp((273 K / T) dG)) maps dG per reactant atom to a positive weight, so exergonic steps are cheap and endergonic steps stay possible (McDermott 2021).

Pathfinding

Yen's k shortest paths on Dijkstra, crossover reactions among intermediates from the phase diagram, then mass-balanced combinations via a pseudoinverse (McDermott 2021).

Filters

The code removes phases above a hull cutoff, interdependent steps and hypothetical intermediates. n = 2 reacting phases keeps the combinatorics in check (McDermott 2021).

Selectivity metrics

Later versions rank single reactions by primary competition among the precursors and secondary competition with the target (McDermott 2023).

Why Na2CO3 selects Y2Mn2O7

Among 3,017 reactions forming Y2Mn2O7 at 650 C, 2 NaMnO2 + 2 YOCl + 1/2 O2 sits on the Pareto front. Its chemical potential distance is 0.055 eV/atom against 1.608 (Li) and 0.924 (K). Todd 2021

Selectivity of BaTiO3 synthesis routes

From 2,536,160 binary reactions among 2417 phases, 82985 BaTiO3 routes were ranked and 9 tested. Metathesis routes gave mainly BaTiO3 at 600 to 700 C. BaCO3 + TiO2 was incomplete at 1000 C. McDermott 2023

How it is used

  1. Choose the system

    Target, precursors, temperature, energy above hull cutoff and any open element such as O2.

  2. Build the network

    Phases from the Materials Project get Gibbs energies, reactions are balanced and weighted.

  3. Find paths

    k shortest paths to each target, crossover reactions, balanced combinations of steps.

  4. Inspect the ranking

    Several low-cost pathways are listed. Compare them with in situ diffraction.
