What AtomisticSkills mat-equation-of-state is

mat-equation-of-state is one of the AtomisticSkills, packaged calculations an agent can call. It wraps matcalc's EOSCalc: the cell is scaled over a range of volumes, the ions are relaxed at each volume with the chosen potential and the energy taken, and a Birch-Murnaghan equation of state is fitted through pymatgen. The fitted B0 is the bulk modulus the skill reports, in GPa. On the map it serves that quantity.

In more detail

A relaxed scan fitted to Birch-Murnaghan

calculate_eos.py hands the structure and the potential to matcalc's EOSCalc with n_points and max_abs_strain. The strain is linear, so the target volumes are (1+e)^3 times V0 for e between minus and plus 0.1 by default, despite matcalc's docstring calling it volumetric. matcalc relaxes every strained point. With allow_shape_change on (matcalc 0.5 or later) the cell shape relaxes at constant volume as well, which is the E(V) a Birch-Murnaghan fit assumes. Symmetry forbids a shape change in cubic cells, so there the flag changes nothing. For anisotropic cells a frozen shape overestimates B0.

Numerical sensitivity of the fit

The pre-relaxation and the per-point relaxations share one fmax, 0.1 eV/A by default. The SKILL.md calls this loose for a cell relaxation and asks for 0.02 to 0.05 eV/A when B0 matters, since a pre-relaxation that stops early moves the whole scan window. V0 from the fit is far less sensitive than B0. The Si example with seven points at plus or minus 8% reaches R^2 = 0.999988 and B0 = 96.43 GPa against about 98 GPa measured, an error the README puts near 2%.

Interfaces and output files

matcalc exposes the Birch-Murnaghan fit as bulk_modulus_bm and r2_score_bm but not the fitted v0 and e0, and its volume and energy keys describe the relaxed input cell. The script therefore refits the scan with pymatgen's BirchMurnaghan and reads v0 and e0 off the fit. For diamond Si at plus or minus 8% strain the relaxed-cell keys lie 0.88 A^3 and 3 meV away from that minimum. The scan itself is saved to energies_volumes.dat and repeated under energy_volume_curve in eos_results.json. The skill runs in the mlip environment for MACE and MatGL and in fairchem for FairChem or UMA.
