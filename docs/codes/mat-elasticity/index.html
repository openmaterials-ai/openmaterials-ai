What AtomisticSkills mat-elasticity is

mat-elasticity is one of the AtomisticSkills, packaged calculations an agent can call. It wraps matcalc's ElasticityCalc: the cell is strained, the stresses are computed with the chosen potential (the skill loads MACE, MatGL or FAIRChem), and the elastic tensor follows. From it the skill reports the bulk, shear and Young's moduli in the Voigt-Reuss-Hill average and the Poisson ratio. On the map it serves those five quantities.

In more detail

Stress-strain fit about a relaxed reference

The skill relaxes the input cell first, since elastic constants are defined about zero stress. matcalc's ElasticityCalc then builds deformed cells with pymatgen's DeformedStructureSet, at normal strains of plus or minus 0.5% and 1% and shear strains of plus or minus 3% and 6% by default. The potential returns a stress per cell and a least-squares regression gives the tensor. With symmetry off, all 21 independent components are fitted. The skill re-minimises the ions in each deformed cell at fixed shape, the relaxed-ion constants the Materials Project and atomate2 compute. matcalc alone defaults to clamped-ion, stiffer by 1.4% on the bulk and 7.5% on the shear modulus of Pnma CaMgSi.

Derived quantities and where hand calculations go wrong

Beyond the VRH averages the script reports the universal anisotropy index A^U = 5 G_V/G_R + B_V/B_R - 6, which needs the Voigt and Reuss bounds kept separate. Directional Young's moduli come from the compliance tensor along [100], [010] and [001] and over all directions, since for CaMgSi the stiffest direction lies about 40 degrees off a in the a-c plane and is 13% stiffer than the stiffest axis. Sound velocities and the Debye temperature follow the Anderson relation with a harmonic-cube mean over the three acoustic branches. Christoffel eigenvalues give the branch velocities along a chosen direction, and the eigenvalues of C_ij give Born stability.

Interfaces and environment

The skill runs through the project's venv/run launcher, in the mlip environment for MACE and MatGL models and in fairchem for FairChem or UMA models. matcalc returns bulk and shear moduli in eV/A^3 and the script converts to GPa with 160.2176634 GPa per eV/A^3. The script computes Young's modulus E = 9BG/(3B+G) and Poisson ratio nu = (3B-2G)/(6B+2G) from the VRH moduli. fmax defaults to 0.1 eV/A. matcalc accepts one threshold for both relaxation stages, so on the relaxed-ion path the script hands it the smaller of --fmax and --deformed_fmax, 1e-4 eV/A by default. Residual forces in a deformed cell would contaminate the fitted stress.
