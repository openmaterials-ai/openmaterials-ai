What xtb is

xtb implements the GFN extended tight-binding methods, GFN2-xTB among them. They give fast energies, geometries, frequencies and properties for molecules and, with GFN0, GFN1 and GFN-FF, periodic systems. On the map it serves the quantities it computes at that level of theory.

In more detail

The GFN methods

The xTB energy is a density-functional perturbation expansion in density fluctuations about neutral spherical atoms, with a partially polarized, mostly minimal valence basis. GFN1-xTB keeps terms to third order with monopole electrostatics, D3 dispersion and a halogen bond correction (Bannwarth 2020). GFN2-xTB adds anisotropic second-order terms through damped cumulative atomic multipoles and the self-consistent D4 dispersion model, and needs no hydrogen or halogen bond corrections (Bannwarth 2019). GFN0-xTB drops the charge iterations for a first-order model, and GFN-FF replaces the zeroth-order tight-binding terms with bond, angle and torsion potentials (Bannwarth 2020).

Numerics and scale

The methods target systems of roughly 1000 atoms and run at least two orders of magnitude faster than ab initio methods (Bannwarth 2019). GFNn-xTB scales cubically with atom count and GFN-FF quadratically. On a quad-core desktop, energies and gradients for proteins of 300 to 6000 atoms show GFN0-xTB 2 to 20 times faster than GFN1 and GFN2, and GFN-FF about two further orders of magnitude faster. ANCopt builds normal mode coordinates from a Lindh model Hessian and combines a rational function step with BFGS updates. GFN2-xTB with GBSA relaxes metal-organic polyhedra of 1100 to 1400 atoms in about one hour, and a Pd30L60 polyhedron of almost 2500 atoms in a few days (Bannwarth 2020).

Distribution and interfaces

xtb is published under the LGPL v3 or later on GitHub, with conda-forge and EasyBuild packages and documentation at xtb-docs.readthedocs.io (Bannwarth 2020). Composite keywords cover optimization, harmonic frequencies and thermodynamic functions, so most calculations need only the geometry. xtb drives crest conformer searches, feeds enso for DFT re-ranking with mRRHO and GBSA corrections, and feeds QCEIMS. The GFN methods are also implemented inside AMS, CP2K, DFTB+, entos, ORCA and TeraChem, with interfaces in ASE and Cuby4. On the map xtb serves molar heat capacities from the THERMO block of an --ohess run, and reaction enthalpies from one such run per species.

Validation

ROT34 rotational constants deviate by 1.24 percent (GFN2), 1.10 percent (GFN1) and 2.50 percent (PM6-D3H4X) in SRD (Bannwarth 2019). For the TMC32 set of 50 transition metal bond lengths the MADs are 5.0 pm for GFN1 and 5.7 pm for GFN2, and across the TMG145 complexes bond lengths deviate by 7.5 and 8.3 pm against about 30 pm for PM6 and PM7. GFN2-xTB averages a 0.9 kcal/mol MAD over eight GMTKN55 noncovalent sets, with 0.6 and 0.2 kcal/mol on HEAVY28 and RG18. On S30L GFN2-xTB reaches 4.0 kcal/mol, close to some large basis DFT-D methods. GFNn-xTB is not recommended for accurate covalent reaction energies and barriers (Bannwarth 2020).
