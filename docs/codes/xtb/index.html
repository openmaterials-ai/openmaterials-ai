What xtb is

xtb implements the GFN extended tight-binding methods, GFN2-xTB among them. They give fast energies, geometries, frequencies and properties for molecules and, with GFN0, GFN1 and GFN-FF, periodic systems. On the map it serves the quantities it computes at that level of theory.
