What ShengBTE is

ShengBTE solves the linearized phonon Boltzmann transport equation from second- and third-order force constants, by the relaxation-time approximation or by iteration. It includes isotope scattering and gives lattice thermal conductivity, phonon lifetimes and mean free paths. On the map it serves those quantities.

In more detail

The iterative Boltzmann solver

ShengBTE writes the linearized BTE for the deviation F of each mode as F = tau0 (v + Delta), where tau0 is the relaxation time and Delta measures the departure from RTA. The zeroth iteration is the RTA, and successive iterations add the repopulation from three-phonon absorption and emission processes and isotope scattering until kappa converges (Li 2014). In Si, where Umklapp scattering dominates, convergence takes 5 steps and adds about 4% to the RTA value. In high conductivity materials with strong normal scattering the RTA treats momentum-conserving processes as resistive and fails. In lonsdaleite the RTA underestimates the diagonal elements by 20% (Li 2014).

Numerics

ShengBTE replaces the Dirac deltas of energy conservation by Gaussians whose width it estimates per scattering process from the group velocity differences and the q grid spacing, times the scalebroad constant. The default is 1.0. For Si the conductivity reaches 90% of its converged value on a 15x15x15 grid at scalebroad 1.0 and on 11x11x11 at 0.1, and fitting kappa(N) to 1 minus exp(-N/A) extrapolates to the dense limit (Li 2014). Point group symmetry reduces the q points. A constrained least squares compensation enforces the sum rules on the anharmonic constants. The lonsdaleite run took about one hour on 32 processors. The code is Fortran 90 with MPI, LAPACK and spglib, GPL-3.0.

Inputs from other codes

ShengBTE computes no force constants. Harmonic constants come from Phonopy (FORCE_CONSTANTS_2ND) or from Quantum ESPRESSO (espresso.ifc2). The companion thirdorder.py generates the minimal set of displaced supercells for the third-order constants by a real-space finite difference scheme and reconstructs the full set from point group, translational and permutation symmetry. Its DFT back end in 2014 was VASP. The number of anharmonic constants is 27 n^3 N^2 for n atoms per cell and N cells, cut by a radius. For many zinc-blende crystals third nearest neighbours suffice (Li 2014). Both sets must use the same functional, or LDA second order with GGA third order overestimates kappa.

Validation

For isotopically pure 28Si the extrapolated conductivity is 172 W/(m K) at room temperature, about 10% above the measured 156 W/(m K), and between the 150 and 165 W/(m K) of an independent calculation. For natural InAs the curve follows experiments over the measured temperature range with scalebroad 0.1, and 0.5 refines it at three times the CPU cost. For lonsdaleite the prediction is 1500 W/(m K) in plane and 1270 W/(m K) along c at 300 K, lower than diamond and graphite in plane (Li 2014). The authors report agreement with experiment better than 10% in the available cases.
