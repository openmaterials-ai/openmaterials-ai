What pymatgen-analysis-diffusion is

pymatgen-analysis-diffusion analyses molecular dynamics trajectories of ionic conductors: mean squared displacements, diffusivities, conductivities and migration pathways. On the map it serves the diffusion quantities it computes.

In more detail

Diffusivity, conductivity and correlation functions

The diffusivity is D = <[dr(t)]^2> / (2dt), from a linear fit of the mean squared displacement against 2dt. Runs at several temperatures give an Arrhenius plot whose slope is the activation energy and whose extrapolation gives D at 300 K, which the Nernst-Einstein equation converts to a conductivity. The probability density P(r), defined on a uniform 3D grid and normalized over the cell, locates sites and migration paths. The van Hove function splits into a self part Gs and a distinct part Gd that reduces to the pair distribution function at t = 0. The probability density and van Hove analyses were implemented in the open-source pymatgen-diffusion add-on (Deng 2016).

Practical settings and automation

AIMD for superionic conductors runs in the NVT ensemble with a Nose-Hoover thermostat at fixed 0 K or experimental cell parameters, non-spin-polarized with a reduced cutoff. A 2 fs time step suffices, and cells of at least 10 A per side limit artificial correlated diffusion from periodic images. Runs take weeks, so a FireWorks workflow adds AIMD jobs per temperature and a custodian WallTimeHandler stops each job before the queue limit. Continuations resubmit until a maximum step count, a required MSD or a required R-squared of the MSD fit is reached. Snapshots and analyses go to MongoDB. With this setup more than 200 compositions were assessed in two years (Deng 2016).

Validation against measured conductors

For argyrodite Li6PS5Cl the stoichiometric cell gives about 2e-3 mS/cm and 524 meV. A Li-excess Li6.25PS5.25Cl0.75 cell gives 14 mS/cm (12 to 18 from the fit uncertainty) and 211 meV, about an order of magnitude above the measured total conductivity of 1.1 mS/cm. The authors list fit uncertainty, grain boundaries in the measured total conductivity and the single conventional cell as causes (Deng 2016). For Na10SnP2S12 the predicted 0.94 mS/cm and 317 meV compare with 0.4 mS/cm and 356 meV measured on the synthesized compound (Richards 2016). For Si-doped Na3PS4 the predicted 1.66 mS/cm compares with 0.74 mS/cm measured for 0.94Na3PS4-0.06Na4SiS4 (Zhu 2015).
