What D3Q is

D3Q extends Quantum ESPRESSO with the third derivative of the energy from density-functional perturbation theory, giving anharmonic force constants without finite displacements. Its thermal transport code solves the Boltzmann equation variationally and computes phonon linewidths. On the map it serves the third-order force constants, the linewidths and the conductivities.

What it computes on the map

5 quantities, each with the interface the representation records. The link opens the quantity on the map.

  • ForceConstants[order=2]d3_q2r.x mat2R (thermal2 format, not flfrc; centered by default, nfar=2), Ry/bohr^2Ry_per_bohr2
  • ForceConstants[order=3]d3_qq2rr.x mat3R (d3_asr3.x, d3_sparse.x refine it), Ry/bohr^3Ry_per_bohr3
  • Linewidth[channel=anharmonic_3ph]d3_lw.x calculation='lw imag' ($prefix_T$T_s$sigma.out), cm^-1inverse_cm
  • ThermalConductivity[bte_solver=direct_inverse]d3_tk.x calculation='cgp' (alias 'exact'; last row of $prefix.$grid_T$T_s$sigma.out), W/(m K)W_per_m_per_K
  • ThermalConductivity[bte_solver=rta]d3_tk.x calculation='sma' ($prefix.$grid_P_sma.out, K_P), W/(m K)W_per_m_per_K
