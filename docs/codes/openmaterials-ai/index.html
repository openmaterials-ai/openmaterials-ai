What openmaterials-ai is

openmaterials-ai is this project's own kernel. It evaluates the closed-form relations the map carries and computes the quantities that have an algebraic definition from their inputs, with no external code. On the map it serves those derived quantities.

In more detail

What the kernel holds

At map version 45be98afae7c the map has 136 typed quantities (122 observable, 11 hidden, 3 parameter), 307 links (265 formula, 42 parameter) and 133 operators in 18 tiers, with representations for 34 codes and 93 committed values, 87 from simulations and 6 from measurements (openmaterials-ai README). The database is plain files. map/ is an append-only log of content-addressed records, and docs/data/ holds the generated graph.json, catalog.json, codes.json, the instances, configurations, models and releases (openmaterials-ai README). The kernel is Apache 2.0 and the map data CC BY 4.0 (openmaterials-ai README).

Closed forms and the dimensional gate

A dimensional gate checks every closed-form edge mechanically. Of the 129 operator edges it walks, 59 are ok, 1 is a documented schematic violation and 69 are skipped as opaque solver edges or non-Eq forms (OpenMaterials document). The proven edges include the Nan random and aligned mixing formulas with the Hasselman-Johnson spherical cross-check, the two cure-kinetics edges, the six thermodynamic identities and the eleven analog-device-metrology closed forms (OpenMaterials document). The cure integral is taken in s = ln(alpha / (1 - alpha)) by 20-point Gauss-Legendre on panels of width 1/2, with the root found by safeguarded Newton (omai.cure_kinetics module).

Interfaces

mapengine and the MaterialsCodeGraph pipeline import the package for record identity, shape and rendering, and the TypeScript worker asserts its own copy against the shipped vectors (openmaterials-ai README). A lineage gzips into a #x= link fragment that opens the playground datasheet with no server, and a committed value has the permalink /l/<id> (openmaterials-ai README). The paper parser turns a PDF into a gated evidence proposal with verbatim quotes, and omai.mermaid exports any sub-map as a Mermaid flowchart (openmaterials-ai README).

Verified layer

105 nodes and 12 edge theorems compile against physlib on Lean 4.31.0, and a generated seven-base extension lifts dimensional coverage to 124 of 132 nodes. 18 identity theorems check against Mathlib, and CI recompiles them on every relevant pull request (OpenMaterials document). These proofs cover dimensions and rational identities, not numerical implementations (openmaterials-ai README).
