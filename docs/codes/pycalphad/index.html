What pycalphad is

pycalphad reads CALPHAD thermodynamic databases and computes Gibbs energies, equilibria and phase diagrams from them. It is the computational core for several alloy design and high-throughput thermodynamics workflows. On the map it serves the thermodynamic quantities it evaluates.
