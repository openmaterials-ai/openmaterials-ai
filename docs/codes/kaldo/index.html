What kALDo is

kALDo computes thermal conductivity from harmonic and anharmonic force constants. It solves the Boltzmann transport equation for crystals and applies the quasi-harmonic Green-Kubo method to disordered and amorphous systems. Both methods take the same inputs, so one code treats crystalline and glassy transport. It runs on GPUs through TensorFlow and accepts force constants from DFT and from interatomic potentials. On the map it serves phonon frequencies, lifetimes, mean free paths and the resulting conductivities.
