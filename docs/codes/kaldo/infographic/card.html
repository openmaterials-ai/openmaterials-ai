kALDo: phonon transport in crystals and glasses from the same force constants

An anharmonic lattice dynamics code that solves the phonon Boltzmann equation for crystals and the quasi-harmonic Green-Kubo equations for disordered solids in one Python workflow.

One theory for order and disorder

Second and third order force constants feed both the Boltzmann equation, solved by RTA, iteration or inversion, and the quasi-harmonic Green-Kubo method for glasses and alloys beyond 1e4 atoms (Barbalinardo 2020).

  • 147W/m/K, silicon at 300 K, quantum BTE, inversionBarbalinardo 2020
  • 17,000atoms in the QHGK Si-Ge clathrate alloy cellBarbalinardo 2020
  • 9960W/m/K, (10,0) nanotube at 300 K, quantum BTEBarbalinardo 2021
Sources: Barbalinardo 2020, Barbalinardo 2021, Zhang 2022, Liu 2025.https://openmaterials.ai/codes/kaldo/
