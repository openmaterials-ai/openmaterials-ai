What Quantum ESPRESSO is

Quantum ESPRESSO is a suite for electronic structure calculations with plane waves and pseudopotentials. pw.x computes total energies, forces and stresses. ph.x computes phonons by density-functional perturbation theory. Further packages cover molecular dynamics, spectroscopy and transport. On the map it serves the ground-state and harmonic quantities.

In more detail

Ground state and linear response

The codes solve the Kohn-Sham equations in a plane-wave basis with norm-conserving, ultrasoft or PAW pseudopotentials. pw.x reaches self-consistency by iterative diagonalization and Broyden mixing, relaxes atoms and cells with BFGS and runs Born-Oppenheimer MD. cp.x performs Car-Parrinello dynamics. ph.x implements density-functional perturbation theory for second and third derivatives of the energy in displacements and electric fields. It gives phonon dispersions, dielectric tensors, Born charges, Raman tensors and electron-phonon coefficients (Giannozzi 2009). Frozen phonons need supercells larger than the force-constant range, at a cost scaling as its ninth power (Baroni 2001).

Numerics and scale

Version 4.1 held about 310,000 lines of Fortran-90 and more than 100 tests (Giannozzi 2009). Parallelism is a hierarchy of MPI communicators over NEB images, k-point pools, plane waves, task groups and dense linear algebra through ScaLAPACK. PWscf scaled to 4800 processors on a gold surface with thiols and water (587 atoms, 2552 electrons), and mixed MPI and OpenMP tests reached 65536 cores (Giannozzi 2009). The GPU port uses CUDA Fortran, cuFFT and cuSOLVER. On a V100 next to an 18-core Xeon the speedup is 1.4x for a 98-atom cell and exceeds 3x as the basis grows, with total energies matching the CPU to 2e-8 Ry and forces to 3e-5 Ry/bohr (Giannozzi 2020).

Interfaces and companion codes

Quantum ESPRESSO is a distribution of separate codes. Its components share the GPL and must be fully interoperable (Giannozzi 2009). XML outputs come with XSD schemas and pseudopotentials use the UPF format (Giannozzi 2020). LAMMPS supplies the classical part of QM-MM. thermo_pw, 3.q and thermal2 add quasi-harmonic and transport properties (Giannozzi 2017). The distribution acts as the quantum engine for i-PI and AiiDA and produces the input of Wannier90, EPW, Yambo, BerkeleyGW, WEST and QMCPack (Giannozzi 2020).

Validation

In the Delta benchmark of 15 solid-state codes with 40 potential or basis set types on 71 elemental crystals, Quantum ESPRESSO agrees with CASTEP to 0.3 meV per atom when both use the Schlipf-Gygi ONCVPSP or the GBRV 1.4 pseudopotentials. Differences between all-electron codes are at most 1 meV per atom (Lejaeghere 2016). The SSSP protocol runs pw.x and ph.x on 85 elements and checks the Delta factor, phonon frequencies, cohesive energies, pressure and band structures. The SSSP efficiency and precision libraries average Delta factors of 0.44 and 0.33 meV, rare-earth nitrides excluded, at average wavefunction cutoffs of 44 and 56 Ry (Prandini 2018).
