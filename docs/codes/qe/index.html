What Quantum ESPRESSO is

Quantum ESPRESSO is a suite for electronic structure calculations with plane waves and pseudopotentials. pw.x computes total energies, forces and stresses. ph.x computes phonons by density-functional perturbation theory. Further packages cover molecular dynamics, spectroscopy and transport. On the map it serves the ground-state and harmonic quantities.
