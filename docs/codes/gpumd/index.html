What GPUMD is

GPUMD runs molecular dynamics on GPUs with many-body and machine-learned potentials. It trains and runs neuroevolution potentials (NEP), and computes thermal transport with the Green-Kubo, homogeneous nonequilibrium (HNEMD) and nonequilibrium (NEMD) methods. It also decomposes the heat current spectrally. On the map it serves the heat current, its autocorrelation, the thermal conductivities derived from them, the NEP fit errors, and the molecular dynamics observables listed below.

In more detail

Many-body forces, virial and heat current on the GPU

GPUMD evaluates many-body potentials through an explicit pairwise force expression. Each atom is one CUDA thread, which accumulates its own force, virial and heat current in a single loop, so no atomic operations are needed (Fan 2017). In the 2017 benchmark on silicon with up to 2,744,000 atoms the speed saturates above 1e5 atoms. Single-precision Stillinger-Weber reached 25 ns per day for 1e6 atoms with a 2 fs step on a Tesla K40, and the double-precision Tersoff speed equalled LAMMPS on about 100 Xeon X5670 cores (Fan 2017). The 2025 release holds about 85,000 lines of CUDA C++ and has been adapted to HIP (Xu 2025).

The neuroevolution potential

NEP is a feed-forward network with one hidden layer on a descriptor whose radial functions are linear combinations of Chebyshev basis functions and whose angular terms add four- and five-body contributions as in the atomic cluster expansion (Fan 2022). The separable natural evolution strategy (SNES) fits the parameters to a loss of energy, force and virial RMSEs with L1 and L2 penalties. On the carbon test set, NEP (4.2 A) reaches 42 meV/atom, 690 meV/A and 160 meV/atom for energy, force and virial at 3,600 atom steps per ms on one V100, against 290 for DP (se2) and 280 for REANN (3 A) (Fan 2022). NEP89, a foundation model for nearly the whole periodic table, ships with GPUMD 4.0 (Xu 2025).

Heat transport methods

GPUMD has three built-in routes. Equilibrium MD uses the Green-Kubo relation on the heat current autocorrelation (compute_hac), HNEMD applies a homogeneous driving force (compute_hnemd), and NEMD uses heat source and sink thermostats (ensemble heat_lan and others). Conductivity and conductance decompose spectrally (compute_shc) and by mode (compute_hnema) (Xu 2025). In the 2022 example, five HNEMD runs of 2 ns each gave 5.1 +/- 0.1 W/(m K) for amorphous carbon at 3.0 g/cm3 and 300 K (Fan 2022). Li 2019 used GPUMD to show that NEMD should report the conductance from the source-sink temperature difference and should use Langevin thermostats (Li 2019).

Files and Python tools

Compilation produces two executables, nep and gpumd (Xu 2025). nep reads nep.in and train.xyz and writes the potential file nep.txt and the loss history in loss.out. gpumd reads run.in and model.xyz and writes thermo.out, hac.out and the other compute outputs (Fan 2022). calorine offers ASE calculators, input and output functions for GPUMD files, and relaxation, phonon and elastic calculations. gpyumd prepares inputs and parses outputs. PyNEP adds descriptor analysis and farthest-point sampling for training sets. NEP_CPU runs the same models on CPUs and inside LAMMPS. The nep-data repository collects published training sets (Xu 2025).

  • MeanSquaredDisplacementrun.in: compute_msd <Nc> <output_interval> → msd.out
  • Potentialnep.txt (default) | EAM/Tersoff/SW potential file referenced from run.in
  • PotentialEnergyFitErrornep, prediction 1 added to the training nep.in, held-out set as train.xyz: energy_train.out, RMSE of column 1 (predicted) vs column 2 (reference), eV/atomev_per_atom
  • PotentialForceFitErrornep, prediction 1 added to the training nep.in, held-out set as train.xyz: force_train.out, RMSE over the 3 pairs (columns 1-3 predicted vs 4-6 reference) of every row, eV/AeV_per_A
  • PotentialUncertaintyExceedance[method=committee_force_spread]run.in: one potential line per committee member (the first drives the MD), then active <check_interval> 0 0 0 <sigma>; fraction of the active.out rows written by this run whose column 2 exceeds sigmadimensionless
  • PotentialVirialFitErrornep, prediction 1 added to the training nep.in, held-out set as train.xyz: virial_train.out, RMSE over the 6 pairs (columns 1-6 predicted vs 7-12 reference), skipping rows whose reference is -1e6 (structure without a virial or stress label), eV/atomev_per_atom

  • ThermalConductivity[transport_model=hnemd]kappa.out (columns kappa_xx, kappa_xy, ..., kappa_zz vs t)
  • Trajectoryrun.in: dump_position <interval>
