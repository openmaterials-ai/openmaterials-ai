Harmonic formula consistency

A separate compiled result proves F = U - T*S directly from OMAI's displayed single-mode free-energy, energy, and entropy formulas. It assumes positive temperature, angular frequency, Boltzmann constant, and reduced Planck constant; Bose occupation is explicitly 1/(exp(x)-1) and zero-point energy is retained. Positive Bose denominators and logarithm arguments are also proved.

This result is not counted as a completed Physlib ensemble binding. Constructing the oscillator ensemble, evaluating its partition function, and proving that the ensemble's energy, entropy, heat capacity, and temperature derivatives match OMAI's formulas remain unproved.

Read the theorem source, formula/source contract, and build record and transitive axiom audit. The contract pins the reviewed formulas and Lean source. It does not automatically translate formulas into proofs.

Evidence and scope

  • Exact definition: the displayed definitions correspond under the specified interpretation. This label alone is not a Lean proof.
  • Conditional: a model, units, index or normalization bridge is required. Inspect the remaining obligations.
  • Conceptual: a related model or concept, not a formula-equivalence claim.
  • Gap: no correspondence was established in the reviewed source. Other or later developments may supply one.

Scalar thermoelectric results require a common operating point and explicit isotropic interpretation where OMAI carries tensor quantities. Harmonic quantities retain zero-point energy, stable positive frequencies, positive temperature, Bose occupation, and their per-mode or cell/molar normalization. Stored linear THz is not angular frequency: omega_SI = 2*pi*10^12*nu_THz.

A direct identity among harmonic closed forms is formula consistency, not the construction and evaluation of an infinite oscillator ensemble. None of these results certifies finite-precision arithmetic, convergence, physical-model adequacy, or a numerical adapter.

Reproduce and inspect

The machine-readable inventory is generated from the reviewed mapping records shipped with OMAI and its existing scientific identities. Current Lean source and build instructions are separate from the retained historical package.

python -m omai.formula_bindings --check
python -m omai.formula_bindings --check --physlib-path /path/to/pinned/physlib
python -m omai.formula_bindings --check --lean-package lean/bridge
cd lean/bridge
lake build --wfail

The read-only source check verifies pinned files and declaration lines. The compiled audit checks fully qualified names and their defining modules. Neither establishes formula equivalence. To reproduce the bridge proofs, build with the pinned Lean dependencies and run the package's axiom audit.
