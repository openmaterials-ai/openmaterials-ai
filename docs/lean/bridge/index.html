Harmonic ensemble proofs

The bridge constructs the full nonnegative-integer quantum harmonic ensemble from Physlib's oscillator spectrum. It evaluates the partition sum and energy moment, proves the required finiteness and integrability, and identifies Physlib's free energy, mean energy, entropy and heat capacity with OMAI's single-mode formulas.

The proofs require positive temperature and angular frequency, use the exact upstream constants, and retain zero-point energy. They also establish dF/dT = -S and dU/dT = C at fixed spectrum. The heat-capacity result transfers the proved energy equality on the positive-temperature domain; it does not assume interchange of an infinite sum and a derivative.

Four further proofs identify OMAI's molar contractions with N_A/N_q times the sum of these single-mode Physlib quantities over a finite grid with 3*N_atoms branches. The basis is a mole of primitive cells. A separate proof constructs the heterogeneous joint ensemble, establishes its product partition and actual F/U/S/C, and connects those quantities to all four molar formulas. Molar normalization is applied after evaluating thermodynamics, not inside the Hamiltonian. Every frequency must be strictly positive; zero acoustic modes require a separate treatment.

Volumetric heat capacity is proved as the mode-capacity sum divided by N_q*V_cell, and separately as the joint ensemble capacity with the same normalization. Volume must be positive and refer to the primitive cell. The SI result is J/(m³ K); an Å³ input requires the exact factor 10^-30. The molar relation is C_vol = C_mol/(N_A*V_cell). The two proofs cover one operator, not two.

Constant-pressure heat capacity

The local static-plus-harmonic bridge proves Cp - Cv = T*V_m*alpha_V^2*K_T on a common primitive-cell molar basis. Cp is defined independently as the temperature derivative of actual ensemble enthalpy along a constant-pressure path. Expansion is V_m'/V_m, and the isothermal bulk modulus is V_m*F_VV for the same molar free energy.

The model attaches the signed static energy N_q*E_0(v) through an actual singleton ensemble, then applies N_A/N_q to the evaluated thermodynamic quantities. Frequencies are positive C2 functions of volume; static energy is C2 and temperature-independent. The reduced-q grid and molar basis stay fixed. The branch must satisfy equilibrium throughout a neighborhood, with positive temperature, volume and curvature at the evaluation point.

A heterogeneous example uses the implicit function theorem to construct a stable branch with nonzero expansion and actual Cp greater than Cv at its reference state. A separate constant-frequency example has zero expansion and Cp equal to Cv. These are exact model examples, not material fits. The result adds one compiled operator/output pair; it does not verify numerical QHA, EOS fits, minimizers, input producers or the sibling compute_heat_capacity_p route.

Inspect the catalog endpoint, actual volume family, two model examples, static ensemble, reviewed endpoint contract, and mapping conditions and sources.

Thermal Grüneisen parameter

The same static-plus-harmonic volume family proves Gamma = alpha_V*K_T/C_V_vol on a local stable constant-pressure branch. Gamma is defined independently as the heat-capacity-weighted mean of mode Grüneisen parameters gamma_i = -v*omega_i'/omega_i, weighted by actual canonical mode heat capacities at the branch volume. Expansion is V_m'(T)/V_m(T) along the branch, the isothermal bulk modulus is V_m*F_VV, and the volumetric heat capacity is sum(c)/(N_q*v), all for the same free energy, temperature and primitive cell.

A supporting result holds at fixed volume without a branch: (dP/dT)_V = Gamma*C_V_vol. The heat-capacity weights use angular SI frequency; mode parameters are logarithmic derivatives and do not depend on a constant frequency scale. Every mode must have positive frequency, so zero acoustic modes need separate treatment. Gamma, the mode parameters and the expansion may be negative, and no statement is made at zero temperature. An adiabatic modulus paired with C_V_vol gives Gamma*(1+alpha_V*Gamma*T), not Gamma.

Exact model examples set the heat-capacity arguments hbar*omega/(kB*T) to 1, 2 and 3 with mode parameters 1, 2 and 3. Gamma then lies strictly between 1 and the arithmetic mean 2: every weight is positive, and the higher-frequency modes, which carry the larger mode parameters, have smaller heat-capacity weights. A negative-rate branch has negative Gamma and negative expansion, and a constant-frequency control with one q point has both equal to zero. These are exact model examples, not material fits. The result adds compiled coverage only for the identity operator and its output; it does not verify FC3 or finite-difference mode-Grüneisen producers, phonopy's fitted thermal-Grüneisen quotient, numerical QHA or input producers. The opaque contract_thermal_gruneisen contraction is covered below, as a corollary of this identity under the quasiharmonic interpretation.

Inspect the catalog endpoint, mode parameters and weights, model examples, actual volume family, reviewed endpoint contract, and mapping conditions and sources.

Quasiharmonic producers

The five quasiharmonic producers are defined from the Gibbs surface of the same static-plus-harmonic ensembles. The Gibbs energy is G(T, p) = inf_V [F(T, V) + p V] over a volume window, the heat capacity is C_P = -T (d2G/dT2)_p, the expansion is alpha_V = (dV/dT)_p / V with V = (dG/dp)_T, the modulus is V (d2F/dV2)_T at that volume, and the thermal Grüneisen contraction is the heat-capacity-weighted mean of the mode parameters.

The C1 implicit-function theorem gives a two-parameter equilibrium family V(T, p) through every stable stationary point. On any volume window across which F + p V is convex, that family is the unique minimizer, so a volume scan certifies its own window; the selected volume must lie strictly inside the scanned range, and extrapolation of a fitted equation of state is not covered. The envelope relations give (dG/dT)_p as -(N_A/N_q) times the Physlib ensemble entropy and (dG/dp)_T = V.

C_P equals the enthalpy derivative of the Cp identity above and also C_V + T (dV/dT)_p (dS/dV)_T, the form of phonopy's default polyfit estimator, through the Maxwell relation (dS/dV)_T = -F_TV = alpha_V K_T; C_P > C_V exactly when alpha_V is nonzero. The expansion read off G is -F_TV/(V F_VV), and the modulus at the selected volume equals -V/(dV/dp)_T; its positivity is the stability premise, not a result. It is the modulus along the model's hydrostatic volume coordinate, not the Voigt average that the BulkModulus node defines. The Grüneisen contraction equals alpha_V K_T / C_V_vol with both read off G. Exact witnesses realize positive, negative and zero expansion.

The theorems hold for any T > 0 with F_VV > 0, including temperatures where the quasiharmonic approximation is physically unreliable. Numerical volume scans, equation-of-state fits, minimizers, FC3 and finite-difference Grüneisen producers and phonopy are not verified; producers must reject imaginary modes at every scanned volume and use one cell for the static energy and the phonons. Inspect OMAIPhyslibGibbsBranch.lean, OMAIPhyslibQHA.lean and OMAIPhyslibQHAWitness.lean, and the conditions for compute_qha_gibbs, compute_heat_capacity_p, compute_thermal_expansion, compute_bulk_modulus_qha and contract_thermal_gruneisen.

Linear elasticity (local proofs)

Physlib has no elastic-solid model, so these are local theorems over Mathlib about the catalog's Voigt contractions K = sum C_aabb / 9 and G = -sum C_aabb / 30 + sum C_abab / 10, its Young's modulus and Poisson ratio, and its equation-of-state modulus V0 E''(V0). The stress C : e is tensile-positive, the negative of the catalog's compressive-positive Stress node.

Any stiffness tensor with the first minor symmetry that stores positive energy for every nonzero symmetric strain has positive Voigt moduli, through an orthogonal basis of deviatoric strains, so the catalog's Poisson ratio lies in (-1, 1/2) for every crystal symmetry. For an anisotropic crystal, E and nu computed from the Voigt moduli are Voigt-polycrystal estimates. An isotropic tensor gives the Lame values K = lam + 2 mu / 3 and G = mu, is stable exactly when both are positive, and gives the Lame expressions for E and nu.

For a cubic crystal the contractions give (C11 + 2 C12)/3 and (C11 - C12 + 3 C44)/5. The trace of the stress depends on the strain only through its trace, tr sigma = 3 K tr e, so the Voigt bulk modulus is the stress-controlled one; the Voigt shear modulus is exact only at Zener ratio 1. With C12 <= C11 and C44 >= 0, the energy minimized over cell shape at fixed volume has curvature V0 E''(V0) = K, which relates the equation-of-state modulus to the tensor contraction. Uniform scaling without shape relaxation gives the Voigt K for every tensor. Inspect OMAIElasticity.lean and the conditions for contract_bulk_modulus.

Composites and cure kinetics (local proofs)

Physlib has no effective-medium or polymer-cure model, so these results are local theorems over Mathlib about the catalog's own formulas. They cite no Physlib declaration and are counted apart from the Physlib-anchored proofs.

Both closed-form branches of the spheroid depolarization factor and its sphere value equal L33(p) = int_0^1 t^2/(t^2 + p^2 (1 - t^2)) dt. So L33 lies in (0, 1), decreases strictly with the aspect ratio p = d3/d1, is continuous through the sphere p = 1 where it equals 1/3, and tends to 0 for needles and to 1 for disks.

For every spheroid shape the random Nan conductivity exceeds the matrix value by k_m f (2 b11 + b33)/(3 - f (2 L11 b11 + L33 b33)) with a positive denominator, so filler whose interface-corrected conductivities both exceed the matrix raises it. For spheres both Nan formulas reduce to Maxwell-Garnett with the corrected filler conductivity k/(1 + 2k/(G d)), which tends to k as the interface conductance grows. Along the alignment axis the aligned conductivity lies strictly between the series and parallel mixtures and, at fixed d3, rises strictly with the aspect ratio.

The catalog's d is a full particle dimension, a sphere's diameter, while Hasselman-Johnson's a_rad is its radius. The Hasselman-Johnson formula is Maxwell-Garnett at d = 2 a_rad, so putting the radius into d doubles the interface penalty. It exceeds the matrix conductivity exactly above the critical radius (k_m/G) k/(k - k_m). Away from spheres the per-direction interface correction is a slab approximation of unvalidated accuracy.

The Kamal-Sourour cure time increases strictly with conversion below full cure and its derivative is the reciprocal rate. Near every interior time there is an inverse alpha(t) that solves d alpha/dt = (k1 + k2 alpha^m)(1 - alpha)^n throughout a neighbourhood; no global solution is claimed. The DiBenedetto relation, in kelvin, maps conversions [0, 1] one to one onto [T_g0, T_ginf] and lies strictly below the linear interpolation inside the interval for lambda < 1.

Inspect OMAICompositeConductivity.lean and OMAICureKinetics.lean in lean/bridge, and the conditions for depolarization_factors, nan_effective_kappa, nan_effective_kappa_aligned, hasselman_johnson, kamal_cure_conversion and dibenedetto_glass_transition.

Lattice dynamics

Physlib's crystal structure supplies the sites and the translation action; the lattice-dynamics content is OMAI-local. Second-order force constants on crystal sites with equal mixed partials, translation invariance and finite range satisfy the index-permutation symmetry Phi_ij(-R) = Phi_ji(R), and the catalog dynamical matrix D_ij(q) = sum_R e^{i q.R} Phi_ij(R)/sqrt(M_i M_j) is Hermitian at every wavevector. With the acoustic sum rule the Gamma-point matrix has at least three independent zero modes, the mass-weighted rigid translations. The catalog uses the cell-vector phase; phonopy's atom-position phase leaves omega^2 and nondegenerate velocities unchanged but changes the eigenvectors by e^{-i q.tau}, so phonopy eigenvectors must not be fed into the catalog's dD/dq.

Real frequencies need mechanical stability. Folding the force constants into a finite Born-von Karman supercell, Phi_BvK(R) = sum_L Phi(R + L), reproduces the catalog D(q) exactly at every wavevector commensurate with the supercell, and a Bloch-wave argument shows that a stable supercell makes D(q) positive semidefinite there, so omega^2 >= 0. Incommensurate wavevectors are the normal case for BTE (a 4x4x4 force-constant supercell with a 19x19x19 mesh), and Fourier interpolation is exact only at commensurate points; nothing is stated at the others.

On a differentiable eigenvector path, away from Gamma, the catalog group velocity e^dagger (dD/dq) e / (2 omega) is real and equals d omega/dq by the Hellmann-Feynman theorem, with dD/dq the exact derivative of the catalog matrix. Symmetry-enforced degeneracies, such as transverse pairs along Gamma-X and Gamma-L, are generic, and the eigenvector a code returns there is not on such a path. A compiled monatomic chain gives omega^2 = (2K/M)(1 - cos(q a)) and velocity a sqrt(K/M) cos(q a / 2).

With the actual Physlib canonical mode heat capacities at T > 0 and positive half-widths Gamma = Im Sigma (so 1/tau = 2 Gamma), the relaxation-time conductivity is symmetric and positive semidefinite, and positive definite exactly when the group velocities span space. The participation ratio of a normalized mass-weighted mode lies between 1/N and 1, with both equality cases; it is a local proof over Mathlib.

Three catalog formulas are recorded as defects and not covered. The non-analytic correction uses the free component q^alpha instead of the contracted q_gamma, so its q dependence cancels. The collision matrix of the linearized BTE has the wrong relaxation-time limit and is not symmetric. The cumulative conductivity takes the linearized-BTE input, for which it is a signed partial sum: a compiled three-mode example with one three-phonon process, Casimir boundary scattering and Bose-Einstein weights is not monotone in either the frequency or the mean-free-path binning. Inspect the dynamical matrix, dispersion, group velocity and relaxation-time conductivity conditions and sources.

Derivatives of an atomistic or cell energy (local proofs)

Physlib has no model of an interatomic potential or a molecular energy surface, so these are local theorems over Mathlib about the catalog's forces, stress, force constants, elastic constants and molecular frequencies, all read as exact derivatives of one energy.

For an energy invariant under rigid translations of all atoms, the catalog forces F = -dE/dR sum to zero in every direction, and the second- and third-order force constants obey the acoustic sum rules over the sites of one index. A real-space grid that breaks translation invariance (the egg-box effect) leaves a nonzero force sum. For an energy invariant under rotations in a plane, as for an isolated molecule but not for atoms rotated inside a fixed periodic lattice, the torque in that plane vanishes. A potential three times continuously differentiable has third-order force constants symmetric under every permutation. A compiled two-atom energy satisfies these hypotheses with a nonzero force and force constant.

The central-difference estimate (F_j(u - h e_i) - F_j(u + h e_i))/(2h) of the finite-displacement force constant is within M h^2 / 6 of the derivative, where M bounds the third derivative of the force along the displacement, the fourth derivative of the potential. One-sided differences have an error of order h and are not covered.

For a cell energy of the deformation gradient that is invariant under rotations of the deformed cell, the compressive-positive catalog stress satisfies sum_b sigma_ab F_cb = sum_b sigma_cb F_ab and is symmetric at the reference cell. At a stress-free reference, for an energy twice continuously differentiable there, the catalog elastic constants have the major symmetry and both minor symmetries; at a pre-stressed reference the minor symmetries fail by residual-stress terms. A stable stress-free reference then has positive Voigt bulk and shear moduli and a Poisson ratio in (-1, 1/2).

Read as the signed square roots of the eigenvalues of the mass-weighted Hessian, the catalog's molecular frequencies have no imaginary mode at a local minimum with positive masses, the catalog's n_imaginary = 0 diagnostic; the converse does not hold. The mass-weighted rigid translations of a translation-invariant potential are zero modes; rotational zero modes are not covered.

The catalog's mode Grüneisen formula is recorded as a defect: it contracts the third-order force constants over two Cartesian indices only, with no eigenvectors, masses, Bloch phases or positions, and leaves three indices free. Inspect OMAIAtomisticDerivatives.lean, OMAICellElasticity.lean and OMAIMolecularModes.lean, and the conditions for compute_forces_hf, compute_stress_cell, compute_force_constants, compute_fc2_finite_displacement, compute_elastic_constants and compute_molecular_frequencies.

Evidence and scope

  • Exact definition: the displayed definitions correspond under the specified interpretation. This label alone is not a Lean proof.
  • Conditional: a model, units, index or normalization bridge is required. Inspect the remaining obligations.
  • Conceptual: a related model or concept, not a formula-equivalence claim.
  • Local: a compiled theorem about the OMAI formula itself, over Mathlib, where Physlib has no model to anchor it. It cites no Physlib declaration. Rows it covers show the status Local proof, not Referenced, and the coverage ledger counts them apart from Physlib-anchored results.
  • Gap: no correspondence was established in the reviewed source. Other or later developments may supply one.

Scalar thermoelectric results require a common operating point and explicit isotropic interpretation where OMAI carries tensor quantities. Harmonic results require stable positive frequencies, positive temperature, Bose occupation, and the stated per-mode or cell/molar normalization. Zero-point energy contributes to free energy and internal energy. Stored linear THz is not angular frequency: omega_SI = 2*pi*10^12*nu_THz.

Temperature results concern an absolute-temperature representation. The pressure result identifies the signed trace under an inviscid constitutive law; it does not prove a DFT stress formula. Reverse navigation identifies references, not mathematical inverse functions. None of these results certifies finite-precision arithmetic, convergence, physical-model adequacy, or a numerical adapter.

Reproduce and inspect

The machine-readable inventory is generated from the reviewed mapping records shipped with OMAI and its existing scientific identities. Reviewed Lean source and build instructions are in lean/bridge.

python -m omai.formula_bindings --check
python -m omai.formula_bindings --check --physlib-path /path/to/pinned/physlib
bash lean/bridge/check.sh
python -m omai.formula_bindings --check --lean-package lean/bridge

The read-only source check verifies pinned files and declaration lines. The compiled source-reference audit checks upstream names and their defining modules. The package audit also checks the endpoint definitions named in the reviewed formula contracts, including definitions retained in earlier modules. These reference and name checks establish provenance, not formula equivalence. The axiom audit requires every bridge source module to be imported with its private kernel data, and includes module-owned private and generated declarations as well as the bridge namespace. It rejects missing modules, incomplete output and unapproved assumptions. Run check.sh to build with the pinned dependencies and perform the endpoint-name and transitive-axiom audits.
